# Builders to watch: open source for West Africa, edition 1

*25 November 2026*

This digest shines a light on open-source projects that serve Nigerians and West Africans, at home and in the diaspora. Each edition credits the people building them, shares what they have shipped, and points to ways you can help. Figures come from public GitHub data for the 90 days to 25 November 2026, gathered by our [scan script](../about.md).

## Spotlights

### Efie Wura Pro, by [nyamebabaku-lab](https://github.com/nyamebabaku-lab)

[nyamebabaku-lab/efiewura](https://github.com/nyamebabaku-lab/efiewura) is rental management for Ghana. Owners can manage properties, units and tenants, sign tenancy agreements electronically, and collect rent and bills paid by MoMo or card. An owner wallet supports withdrawals to MoMo or a bank account. It also covers repairs, expenses, SMS and reports. It helps property owners and managers run their rentals, and gives tenants a simple way to pay and report repairs.

- 11 commits in the last 90 days, and six Android app releases (android-v1.0.2 to android-v1.0.7)
- Last activity: 11 November 2026
- Licence: none yet

**How you can help:** try the [live demo](https://nyamebabaku-lab.github.io/efiewura/demo/), star the repo, and open an issue with your feedback. Adding an open-source licence would make it easier for others to use the project and contribute.

### Season Margin, by [batestguy](https://github.com/batestguy)

[batestguy/crop-value-predictor](https://github.com/batestguy/crop-value-predictor) is Season Margin, a crop comparison tool for Nigerian farmers. Its motto is "Know your margin before you plant." You enter your own land, expected yield, selling price and costs, and it compares a shortlist of crops side by side, ranked by net profit. Prices are shown in naira per kilogram and per bag. An optional research button suggests starting prices and yields for your state, but nothing changes until you accept it. The calculator works offline once loaded, and your drafts stay on your device. It helps farmers see what a season could earn before they commit seed and money.

- 71 commits in the last 90 days, including the rename to Season Margin, a walkthrough video, new screenshots and an MIT licence
- Last activity: 23 November 2026
- Licence: MIT

**How you can help:** try the [live app](https://crop-value-predictor.pages.dev/) on your phone or computer, star the repo, and open an issue if local prices or yields in your area look different.

### Hausevo, by [thatmanfrancis](https://github.com/thatmanfrancis)

[thatmanfrancis/Hausevo](https://github.com/thatmanfrancis/Hausevo) is a property platform for the Nigerian rental market, inspired by problems in the Lagos housing market. Tenants can browse homes, apply, track rent schedules and raise maintenance requests or disputes. Landlords can oversee properties and tenancies, and artisans can manage assigned repair work. Property scouts can submit listings on a landlord's behalf. It helps tenants, landlords and artisans keep a shared record of a home, from discovery to repairs.

- 1 commit in the last 90 days (a README update)
- Last activity: 22 November 2026
- Licence: none yet

**How you can help:** visit [hausevo.com.ng](https://hausevo.com.ng), star the repo, and open an issue with ideas or bugs. Adding an open-source licence would make it easier for others to use the project and contribute.

### Nigeria land registry blockchain prototype, by [amirofekiti](https://github.com/amirofekiti)

[amirofekiti/Nigeria-Land-Registry-Blockchain-Prototype](https://github.com/amirofekiti/Nigeria-Land-Registry-Blockchain-Prototype) is a research prototype that supports a manuscript on secure, auditable property registration in Nigeria. It uses a blockchain as an audit and record-keeping layer alongside the official land registry, not as a replacement for it, and it does not treat a token or wallet address as legal title. The README records functional and security testing and an evaluation on the Ethereum Sepolia test network. It is a research artefact, not a live land registry. Work like this helps researchers and policymakers explore how land records could become easier to trust and audit.

- 49 commits in the last 90 days, all on 18 and 19 September 2026, covering the smart contract, security tests and the Sepolia evaluation
- Last activity: 19 September 2026
- Licence: none yet

**How you can help:** read the evaluation notes in the repo, star it, and open an issue with questions or ideas. Adding an open-source licence would make it easier for others to use the project and contribute.

## Also shipping

Open-source building blocks for mapping, field data, farming, lending and food prices. Commit counts are on the default branch over the last 90 days.

- [frappe/lending](https://github.com/frappe/lending): open-source lending software. 655 commits.
