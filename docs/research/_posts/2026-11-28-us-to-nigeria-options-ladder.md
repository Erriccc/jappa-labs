*As of 28 November 2026.*

Someone in the US wants to get an item to a home in Nigeria. It might be one phone or a full 50 lb suitcase. This note sets out the realistic routes, roughly what each costs delivered to a Lagos door, and when it is cheaper to buy the item in Lagos instead.

## The routes

- **Buy in Lagos.** Same day, but fakes are a risk and local prices are hard to pin down.
- **A relative's or your own bag.** Often the cheapest, but someone has to be flying, and airline fees vary.
- **Nigerian forwarders and consolidators.** Air takes one to two weeks and sea takes six to eight. Most ask for a content list, and some add a handling charge for new gadgets.
- **US Postal Service.** Simple to use, but insurance is reportedly not available to Nigeria.
- **Express couriers.** Three to eight days, at the top of the price range.
- **Amazon shipping direct.** Amazon decides item by item, and electronics are often blocked.

The [options ladder](../../guide/options-ladder.md) explains when each route wins. The [size classes](../../guide/size-classes.md) show how each size is billed, and the city guides for [Chicago](../../guide/scenarios/chicago.md), [DC and Baltimore](../../guide/scenarios/dc-baltimore.md), [Houston and Texas](../../guide/scenarios/houston-texas.md) and [Atlanta](../../guide/scenarios/atlanta.md) give the expected best route for each size. ## What we found

- Below a few pounds, most forwarders charge a minimum or a flat fee, so small items cost about the same whatever they weigh.
- From a carry-on bag upwards, per-pound air rates decide the cost, and sea freight is far cheaper if you can wait.
- Bulky but light items, such as an air fryer, can be billed on box size rather than weight. Measure your carton before you compare.
- For phones and tablets, buying in Lagos and sending from the US came out roughly even once shipping and duty were added. Those Lagos prices are provisional.
- We found no published parcel rate from Lagos to other states, such as to Owerri.

## Still open

Like-for-like Lagos prices, the duty rate on phones and laptops, and rates beyond Lagos all still need confirming.

The full sourced notes, with every figure and how it was worked out, are in [issue #12](https://github.com/Erriccc/jappa-labs/issues/12).

This is general information, not legal, customs or tax advice. Prices and rules change, so confirm with the carrier or forwarder before you ship.
