*As of 28 November 2026.*

Which companies move US purchases to Nigeria, how big they are, and which have shut down. It covers forwarders, Shop & Ship services and buy-for-me services on the US to Nigeria route.

## Active

- **Global forwarders**, such as MyUS, give you a US address and ship worldwide.
- **Nigerian services**, such as Topship, Shoptomydoor, Skyshop, SGK Global and BigShipment, give you a US address or pickup and deliver to a door in Nigeria. Some include Lagos clearance or duty in the price.
- **Buy-for-me services**, such as JOBOX and Beta-eshopping, buy the item for you and ship it.

The guide's [provider directory](../../guide/providers.md) lists these services by type, in alphabetical order, with no paid placement.

## Closed or stalled

- **Heroshe** had long delivery delays after moving its warehouse in 2025, and is reported to have stopped operating. We found no statement from the company.
- **Shypmate**, a traveller-delivery service, renamed itself Envyl, moved its focus to Ghana and is now listed as closed.
- **Grabr**, a global traveller-shopping marketplace, looks inactive. Its status is unconfirmed.
- **PiggyBee**, a traveller-carry platform with no insurance, closed in 2020.

Before you send, check that a service is still operating, on its own site or app.
