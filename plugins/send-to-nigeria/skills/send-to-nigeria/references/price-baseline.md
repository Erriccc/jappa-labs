# Price baseline per provider, US to Nigeria

All figures here were collected **as of 28 Nov 2026**. Where a provider's page carried its own date, it is shown as "page date". Prices change: re-check live before quoting (see [recheck-live.md](recheck-live.md)).

Every figure in this file sits next to its source URL. Figures from the research with **no recorded URL** are not in these tables. They are listed at the end and must be re-found with WebSearch before quoting.

Labels:
- **[fetched]**: page read.
- **[decoded]**: PDF text extracted.
- **[snippet]**: search summary only; page not read.
- **[stale]**: older or superseded.
- **[unverified]**: inconsistent or third-party.
