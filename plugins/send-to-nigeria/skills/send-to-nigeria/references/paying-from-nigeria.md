**FX markup:** about 1.5–2% for Cardtonic, Bitnob and Eversend [snippet; URL not recorded].

**Tip for comparing:** Non-US-merchant fees (Eversend, Cleva) are charged on merchants outside the US. Whether a given store or forwarder counts as a US merchant is not stated anywhere; that part is our inference, so check with the card provider. Add the create fee, the funding fee, any per-transaction fee and the FX markup to the landed cost.

## Naira cards [snippet]

Sources: BusinessDay, https://businessday.ng/pro/article/banks-ease-overseas-spending-limits-as-fx-crunch-fades/ ; Legit.ng, https://www.legit.ng/business-economy/money/1724047-nigerian-banks-increase-international-card-spending-limits/ . Still to open: Nairametrics, 7 Sep 2026 (URL not recorded).

- **No single CBN cap was found.** Each bank sets its own foreign-spend limit, and most raised them in 2026.
- **GTBank:** $20,000/quarter (earlier $6,000/quarter, and $1,000 in Jul 2025; a $40k figure is unverified). [snippet; BusinessDay or Legit.ng above; which article gave this bank's figure: URL not recorded]
- **FirstBank:** $10,000/quarter. [snippet; BusinessDay or Legit.ng above; which article gave this bank's figure: URL not recorded]
- **Zenith:** up to $50,000/year. [snippet; BusinessDay or Legit.ng above; which article gave this bank's figure: URL not recorded]
- **Stanbic:** $8,000/quarter. [snippet; BusinessDay or Legit.ng above; which article gave this bank's figure: URL not recorded]
- **Fidelity:** no international use on naira cards.
- **Amazon often declines naira cards.** Common causes are international use switched off, a billing-address mismatch, or fraud filters.

## Dollar debit cards on domiciliary accounts [snippet]

- Offered by GTBank, Zenith, Access and UBA.
- GTBank reportedly has no online limit.

## Other routes [snippet]

- **Payoneer:** mainly for receiving money; the prepaid card costs $29.95/yr [snippet; URL not recorded].
- **USDT:** Amazon doesn't take crypto. People use USDT to fund virtual cards.
- **Gift cards:** bought from resellers, with a conversion cost.
- **Amazon.com payment methods:** Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover and gift cards. Not confirmed on an Amazon page.
- **Paying a forwarder in Nigeria:**
  - Aramex Shop & Ship takes naira.
  - Hont adds 7.5% VAT on payments made in Nigeria [decoded, April 2026 PDF, https://www.hontglobal.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Hont-Global-Services-Shipping-Details-_April-2026.pdf].

## Buy-for-me services (someone buys for you) [snippet]

Instead of paying the US store yourself:
- **Nigerian Shop:** about 5% (old figure) [snippet; URL not recorded].
- **Cargome:** flat $35–55 plus shipping [snippet; URL not recorded].
- **Dechaser (FCEL):** FCEL air rates (see the FCEL row in [price-baseline.md](price-baseline.md)) plus a concierge fee; the concierge fee amount is not recorded.
- **US2NAIJA:** quote per order.

The 10–15% figure sometimes quoted belongs to TryShopper [snippet] https://tryshopper.com/ , a local Nigerian errand service, not a US buy-for-you rate.
