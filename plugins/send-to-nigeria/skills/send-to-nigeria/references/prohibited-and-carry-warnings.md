# Prohibited items and carry-for-strangers warnings

Research date: **28 Nov 2026**. **This is not legal or customs advice.** Lists are partial and change. Always check the carrier's current list and the Nigeria Customs Service before sending.

Research behind this file:
- sending rules, insurance and bags: https://github.com/Erriccc/jappa-labs/issues/16
- traveller carry: Carryam4me's guarantee terms, https://carryam4me.com/

## Nigeria import prohibition list (as reported)

Reported items [snippet, secondary; the Customs Act text was not read]:
- poultry, pork and beef;
- refined vegetable oil;
- used tyres;
- **footwear and bags**;
- **used clothing**.

**Rice is not on the list.**

The research did **not** resolve how these items are treated in personal shipments and baggage, as opposed to commercial imports. This matters for:
- sneakers and handbags;
- **suitcases or barrels of used clothes**.

If the user's goods include them, flag it and tell them to confirm with the forwarder before sending.

## Carrier prohibited lists

**Topship** [snippet]: https://topship.africa/prohibited-items
- perfumes, spices, dairy, yam, beef/ponmo;
- luxury branded goods;
- currency;
- hazardous materials;
- lithium metal batteries on the Saver service.

The list is non-exhaustive.

**SGK Global** [fetched]: https://sgkglobal.com/shipping-barrels-to-nigeria/
- Narcotics, weapons and counterfeits are prohibited.
- Commercial food needs NAFDAC approval.
- A full content list is required at intake.
- Drums must be rigid, with sealable lids.
- Banned items can cause the **whole container** to be forfeited [snippet].

**Hont** [fetched]: https://www.hontglobal.com/faq/
- Ships perfume and dangerous goods (DG), but at a higher rate: $9.00/lb Lagos, $10.30/lb outside Lagos, plus a $20 documentation fee [fetched] https://www.hontglobal.com/faq/ .

**Hont, April 2026 PDF** [decoded]: https://www.hontglobal.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Hont-Global-Services-Shipping-Details-_April-2026.pdf
- Customers must provide receipts or invoices.
- Government photo ID is required.
- Collection needs ID, and proxies must be named in advance.

**Other carriers:** Konga Logistics and TNT Nigeria reserve the right to open shipments [snippet].

## Airline rules on checked bags

**Turkish** [snippet]: the Conditions of Carriage reportedly:
- bar accepting baggage from, or entrusted by, third parties;
- bar electronics, money and jewellery in checked bags.

**Qatar** [snippet]: bars valuables and electronics in checked bags.

**Ethiopian** [fetched]: "please do not accept any items from anyone as you will be held responsible and accountable for the contents of any of the items found in your custody."

**Delta** [fetched, partial]: baggage is the passenger's property. No ban on carrying for others was found in the roughly 83% of the text that was read.

## Carrying for strangers: the warnings

Tell anyone thinking of carrying a package for someone they don't know well:

1. **You are personally liable for what is inside.** With cargo, the documented exposure is seizure plus a penalty on the declarant. With a bag you carry, **the carrier is arrested in person**. Under NDLEA Act s.11(a), importing hard drugs carries **life imprisonment** [snippet].
2. **Airlines tell you not to.** See Ethiopian's wording above, and Turkish's reported clause.
3. **Insurance won't cover it.** Off-the-shelf parcel insurance doesn't cover a hand-carried suitcase to Lagos. Shipsurance excludes Nigeria, hand delivery, and transport by anything other than a commercial carrier [fetched]. UPS Capital reportedly doesn't cover Nigeria [snippet].
4. **Platform guarantees are small and conditional.** Carryam4me's self-funded guarantee is up to £150 [fetched]. It excludes undeclared or prohibited items and customs seizure, and it needs photos or video at handoff.
5. **If you do carry for someone you know**, these steps are research suggestions, not an industry standard:
   - Open and inspect everything.
   - Never take a sealed package.
   - Keep the receipts.
   - Photograph the contents.
   - Make sure the total stays within your duty-free allowance and your bag allowance.

## Value and declaration

- **Never under-declare value.** False statements on a Nigerian customs declaration reportedly carry **₦3,000,000 or up to 3 years in prison** [snippet; section number unconfirmed].
- **Never suggest splitting a cart** to stay under the $300 duty-free limit (see [nigeria-duty.md](nigeria-duty.md)).
- **Declare value when insuring.** Hont caps compensation on undeclared shipments at $100 [decoded] https://www.hontglobal.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Hont-Global-Services-Shipping-Details-_April-2026.pdf .

## Amazon blocks (not prohibitions, but they stop a route)

Amazon decides per item whether it ships to Nigeria. Electronics, some brands and US-only third-party sellers are often blocked (Getcleva, Apr 2026) [fetched]: https://www.getcleva.com/blog/amazon-shopping-and-shipping-to-nigeria-in-2026-how-does-it-work

Phones are likely blocked [inference; not tested].
