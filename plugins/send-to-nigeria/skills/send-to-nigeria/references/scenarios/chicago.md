# Shipping from Chicago to Nigeria

Scenario file: one section per size class (see [size-classes.md](../size-classes.md)). Baseline **as of 28 Nov 2026**. Every figure carries its label, source URL and date. "URL not recorded" means the research named the source but kept no link. **Re-check live before quoting** ([recheck-live.md](../recheck-live.md)).

This is not legal or customs advice.

**Assumptions for every section:**
- Destination is **Lagos** unless a row says otherwise. Other states cost more.
- Duty: under $300 per consignment, with the person's one duty-free import this quarter unused, duty is $0. See [nigeria-duty.md](../nigeria-duty.md).
- **Online order** rows: the store ships to the forwarder's US address, so the sender's city does not matter.
- **Local** rows: someone in this city already has the goods.

## About this city

- **Local options found:** SGK Global (scheduled pickups across the Chicago metro and northwest Indiana; pickup fee exists but is not stated) and County Cargo (Chicago-based, snippet only).
- **Not local:** Cargo Naija lists Atlanta, Houston, Texas, Maryland and New York, not Chicago. Hont ships from Houston only.
- **Nonstop flights to Lagos:** none in the research data (BTS rows covered ATL, IAD and JFK only).

## Where geography changes the answer

- SGK air from Chicago flies out of O'Hare (ORD), so small and medium air parcels do not need a leg to Texas [fetched, https://sgkglobal.com/shipping-from-chicago-to-nigeria, as of 28 Nov 2026].
- SGK barrels and sea freight go through Stafford TX [fetched, https://sgkglobal.com/shipping-barrels-to-nigeria/, page 25 Sep 2026]. Whether the Chicago pickup covers barrels, and what the first-mile leg to Texas costs, is **not found**.
- Hont (Houston): a Chicago sender would have to get the goods to Houston first. The cost of that domestic leg is **not found**.
