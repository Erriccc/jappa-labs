# Shipping from Atlanta and Georgia to Nigeria

Scenario file: one section per size class (see [size-classes.md](../size-classes.md)). Baseline **as of 28 Nov 2026**. Every figure carries its label, source URL and date. "URL not recorded" means the research named the source but kept no link. **Re-check live before quoting** ([recheck-live.md](../recheck-live.md)).

This is not legal or customs advice.

**Assumptions for every section:**
- Destination is **Lagos** unless a row says otherwise. Other states cost more.
- Duty: under $300 per consignment, with the person's one duty-free import this quarter unused, duty is $0. See [nigeria-duty.md](../nigeria-duty.md).
- **Online order** rows: the store ships to the forwarder's US address, so the sender's city does not matter.
- **Local** rows: someone in this city already has the goods.

## About this city

- **Local options found:** SGK Global (pickup across metro Atlanta, trucked to Stafford TX in 2–3 business days); Cargo2Lagos (BlueLine, Tucker GA; snippet, undated); FCEL/Dechaser (Atlanta rate card, page dated 20 Jan 2026); Hezelinks (unverified); Cargo Naija (lists Atlanta).
- **Nonstop flights to Lagos:** Delta ATL–LOS (BTS T-100, Dec 2024 and Dec 2025).

## Where geography changes the answer

- SGK trucks Atlanta pickups to Stafford TX in 2–3 business days [fetched, https://sgkglobal.com/shipping-from-atlanta-to-nigeria, as of 28 Nov 2026]. The first-mile leg is part of SGK's pickup service. The pickup fee is quoted per ZIP code and its amount is **not found**.
- The Delta ATL–LOS nonstop makes the relative-carries-it route realistic from Atlanta.
- FCEL's $8.79/lb is for collection at its Lagos office, not door delivery.
