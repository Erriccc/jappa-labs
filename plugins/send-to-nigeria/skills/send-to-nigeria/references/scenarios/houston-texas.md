# Shipping from Houston and Texas to Nigeria

Scenario file: one section per size class (see [size-classes.md](../size-classes.md)). Baseline **as of 28 Nov 2026**. Every figure carries its label, source URL and date. "URL not recorded" means the research named the source but kept no link. **Re-check live before quoting** ([recheck-live.md](../recheck-live.md)).

This is not legal or customs advice.

**Assumptions for every section:**
- Destination is **Lagos** unless a row says otherwise. Other states cost more.
- Duty: under $300 per consignment, with the person's one duty-free import this quarter unused, duty is $0. See [nigeria-duty.md](../nigeria-duty.md).
- **Online order** rows: the store ships to the forwarder's US address, so the sender's city does not matter.
- **Local** rows: someone in this city already has the goods.

## About this city

- **Local options found:** SGK Global (warehouse at 4910 Wright Road, Suite 160, Stafford, TX 77477; drop-off is free) and Hont (weekly Friday air from IAH). BigShipment /USA lists Houston rates, likely from an older page. Cargo Naija lists Houston and Texas.
- **Nonstop flights to Lagos:** none in the research data (BTS rows covered ATL, IAD and JFK only).

## Where geography changes the answer

- Houston is SGK's base and the origin port for its sea and barrel services [fetched, https://sgkglobal.com/shipping-barrels-to-nigeria/, page 25 Sep 2026]. Senders here pay **no first-mile leg to Texas**: free drop-off at Stafford avoids SGK's per-ZIP pickup fee.
- Hont flies from IAH and also ships by sea [fetched, https://www.hontglobal.com/faq/, as of 28 Nov 2026].
