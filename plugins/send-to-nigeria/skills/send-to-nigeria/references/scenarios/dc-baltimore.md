# Shipping from Washington DC, Baltimore and Northern Virginia to Nigeria

Scenario file: one section per size class (see [size-classes.md](../size-classes.md)). Baseline **as of 28 Nov 2026**. Every figure carries its label, source URL and date. "URL not recorded" means the research named the source but kept no link. **Re-check live before quoting** ([recheck-live.md](../recheck-live.md)).

This is not legal or customs advice.

**Assumptions for every section:**
- Destination is **Lagos** unless a row says otherwise. Other states cost more.
- Duty: under $300 per consignment, with the person's one duty-free import this quarter unused, duty is $0. See [nigeria-duty.md](../nigeria-duty.md).
- **Online order** rows: the store ships to the forwarder's US address, so the sender's city does not matter.
- **Local** rows: someone in this city already has the goods.

## About this city

- **Local options found:** Icon Logistics in Laurel MD (publishes no prices; quotes "in one business day"; pickup anywhere in the US, same-week in MD/DC/VA; drop-off at Laurel; Lagos only). Hezelinks has a Maryland page (unverified). Cargo Naija lists Maryland.
- **No forwarder publishes DC- or Virginia-specific prices.** SGK's Baltimore page returned 404, and SGK pickup for DC/VA is unconfirmed.
- **Nonstop flights to Lagos:** United IAD–LOS; Delta JFK–LOS nearby (BTS T-100). Baltimore has no nonstop of its own.

## Where geography changes the answer

- SGK's barrels and sea freight leave from Stafford TX [fetched, https://sgkglobal.com/shipping-barrels-to-nigeria/, page 25 Sep 2026]. Heavy goods from DC or Baltimore would pay a first-mile leg to Texas. That cost is **not found**.
- Icon is the local heavy-goods option: barrels go 22–35 days port to port, and RoRo (vehicles) runs Baltimore to Lagos in 21–26 days. Air consolidations run every two weeks from JFK, IAD or ATL [fetched, https://iconshipping.com/barrel-shipping ; https://iconshipping.com/ship-from-baltimore ; https://iconshipping.com/west-africa-consolidation, undated].
- The IAD–LOS nonstop makes the relative-carries-it route realistic from this area.
